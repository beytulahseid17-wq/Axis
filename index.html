Privacy Policy

Axis stores your data in your own account with row-level security — no other user can access it. Financial figures are manual and never connected to real bank accounts. AI coach messages go to Google Gemini only to generate your reply.

Terms & Conditions

Axis is provided as-is. Financial figures and trip plans are for personal organisation only. AI suggestions may be inaccurate — use your own judgment.

FAQ

Is my data private?
Yes — isolated at the database level by row-level security.

Does Axis connect to my bank?
No — all figures are entered manually.

Works offline?
Yes — installable as a PWA, caches itself for offline use.

Danger zone

Deleting your account removes your data permanently. This requests deletion — an admin process finalizes it within 30 days.
